Fish Fillets 4ever is an independent, open-source port of Fish Fillets, created by ALTAR in 1998. The original Delphi source is translated into a modern engine, keeping the puzzles, physics, and peculiar sense of humor intact.

Keep the classic look, or choose enhanced and AI-upscaled artwork. The scenery gets a fresh coat of paint; the challenge stays the same.

Ported using TypeScript, with WebGL rendering. Explore the source on GitHub.
