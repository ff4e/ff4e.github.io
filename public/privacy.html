Privacy policy

Fish Fillets Forever (shown as Fishes on iOS) and the Fish Fillets 4ever website.

Last updated: September 13, 2026

The game's maintainer can be contacted at fish_fillets@icloud.com.

Playing on iPhone or iPad

The iOS release bundles the game and its artwork, music, voices and videos. Playing does not require an account or an internet connection. This release does not enable web analytics or automatically send gameplay, advertising identifiers or bug reports to the maintainer. This release contains no advertisements and does not track you across apps for advertising.

Progress, saved games, move records, scores, play time and settings are stored locally on your device. The game does not implement iCloud save synchronization. Device backups and Apple's own services are managed separately by Apple and your device settings, not by the game's maintainer.

Optional feedback

If you open the feedback form, it prepares a report on your device and shows you its contents before you choose to share it. Opening or typing in the form does not send the report. You can close it without sharing anything.

A bug report can contain your description; app version, build hash and build date; current screen and room number, name and title; graphics tier, renderer and WebGL availability; subtitle setting; viewport and screen dimensions; browser or web-view user-agent information; move count and the current room's move record. An idea report contains your text and build information, without the room or device diagnostics. Reports do not automatically include your other saved games or complete progress history.

  • GitHub: choosing "Open a GitHub issue" sends the prefilled report to GitHub in the link, even if you do not ultimately submit the issue. Submitting publishes it in the project's public issue tracker alongside your GitHub account identity. Other people can read and copy public issues.
  • Email: choosing "Send an email" hands a draft to your mail application. If you send it, the maintainer receives the report and the sender name and email address supplied by your mail account. Your mail provider and the recipient's iCloud Mail service also handle the message.
  • Copy report: this copies the report to your clipboard. It does not submit it to the maintainer; any later sharing depends on where you paste it and what you do there.

Do not include passwords, private documents or other sensitive information. Use email, not a public issue, for a private support or privacy request.

How shared reports are used and kept

The maintainer uses reports to respond to requests, reproduce problems and improve the game, not for advertising, selling personal data or building profiles across other apps.

Email reports are retained while needed to handle the request and follow-up work. Public issues can remain as part of the project's development history. You can request access, correction or deletion of information held by the maintainer by emailing fish_fillets@icloud.com. Copies held independently by GitHub, mail providers or other readers are subject to their own controls and retention policies.

The website and external links

The browser version downloads game assets from the website, unlike the bundled iOS app. Browser progress and settings stay in that browser's local storage. GitHub Pages hosts the website, including this policy, and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Website builds may enable Cloudflare Web Analytics for visit counts and performance measurements. This is separate from optional feedback and is not enabled in the iOS release. See Cloudflare's Web Analytics information. This policy page itself loads no analytics scripts, remote fonts or images.

Opening the policy, a GitHub link or another external website makes a request to that service. Those services handle connection and account information under their own policies. Apple's App Store, TestFlight and device services are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Your choices

You can play without submitting feedback. Clear the website's site data to remove browser saves and settings, or delete the iOS app and its data to remove its local saves; offloading an app is not the same as deleting its data. These actions erase local progress and do not delete reports already shared. For those reports, contact the maintainer at the address above.

Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
