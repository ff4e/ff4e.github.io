Furniture

72/66 is what ships today and is explicitly not final. Measured: a LEFT strip is nearly free at any size (a phone pays 0.49% at 72px, 0.66% at 96px), a TOP strip costs ~0.29% of the room per pixel. So size the left one for thumbs and the top one as thin as the icons allow. A TV strip holds a legend, not touch targets, so it can be thinner still.

Margin

VIEWPORT_MARGIN, and it drives both frames. 0 on PC and touch: nothing can overflow without it, so it buys air and costs about 2×margin/height of room size. TV defaults to 27 — a title-safe inset is this same reserve.
