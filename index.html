Fish Fillets 4ever doesn't fit on a phone

The game is played with a mouse and a keyboard — you click a fish to pick it, click the water to swim there, right-click to shove, and the keys do the rest. There is no touch control scheme, and on a phone-sized screen the controls are too small to hit.

Come back on a computer — or a tablet, which is just big enough — and it will be waiting.

Running on the software renderer (WebGL unavailable) — the game may run slower, and in the AI-upscaled tier the water is drawn at the original resolution.
